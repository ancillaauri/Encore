      Advanced

      Use with care. This puts Encore's recording ahead of everything else on your graphics card, including the game and Windows. While it's on, other programs that use the graphics card, on any screen, may slow down or freeze. On a PC with an unstable graphics driver it can also make the screen go black for a moment, or freeze the PC. If that happens, turn it off. Works best with Start Encore as administrator on.

      Changelog

      Before you turn on Priority Capture

      Priority Capture puts Encore's recording ahead of everything else on your graphics card, including your game. Use it for games that put a heavy load on your graphics card, or if your graphics card is less powerful, so recordings and Echoes don't fall behind.

      While it's on, other programs that use the graphics card, such as videos, streams or games on your other screens, may slow down, stutter or freeze until you turn it off.

      On a PC with an unstable graphics driver it can also make the screen go black for a moment, or freeze the PC. If that happens, turn it off.

      It turns itself off whenever Encore starts, once no game has been detected for a minute, and if the capture hits an error.
