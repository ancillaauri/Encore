## Encore 0.8.2 (beta), test build 18: new layout

### What's changed
- The record button sits higher.
- **Select Application** and **Game** are on their own row under the screens (Follow and your
  monitors).
- In small windows everything shrinks to fit, top to bottom as well as side to side.
- From 0.8.1: Follow has its own tile, picking a monitor always records it, and One app is now
  Select Application.

### Install
Update from inside Encore (≡ → Help → Update to 0.8.2).
