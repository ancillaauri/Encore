## Encore 0.8.3 (beta), test build 19: new top area

### What's changed
- **Switches** on the left of the record button: Instant replay is now here (Length and Save
  replay stay in the side panel). Game detection and Do not disturb are shown greyed out until
  they arrive.
- **A cleaner record button**: just the dot (a square while recording). The timer and
  "Start recording · Ctrl + Alt + R" sit underneath.
- **Status** on the right: what Encore is doing, at a glance.
  - Standing by / Recording / Saving
  - Following you (and which screen) / Locked to screen / Application
  - Instant replay: holding the last 2 minutes, or off
  - Audio tracks while recording
  - Where recordings are saved
- A divider line, fading at both ends, separates the top area from **What to record**.

### Install
Update from inside Encore (≡ → Help → Update to 0.8.3).
